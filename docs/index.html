All eleven stages and how to earn the 10th and 11th, the secret entrances, the four ships with every weapon firing, the enemies, the bosses, the story and the ending animated, the pause passwords and the 26 tunes taken from its own engine.

Konami - (c) Konami 1988 - Cartridge RC-764, 256 KB MegaROM (Konami with SCC) - MSX - Dump 7393f677...

There are no screenshots here. The stage routes, the ship and the sprites of every stage are drawn from the bytes of the ROM and checked against openMSX's VRAM; the animations, the weapons and the bosses are the VRAM of every frame of a game in openMSX, drawn as the TMS9918 draws it.

What is inside

The game

  • The stages: the eleven stages, their order, the secret entrances and every route whole.
  • The ship: the four ships, the weapon bar and every weapon firing.
  • The enemies: the sprites of every stage, with their colour.
  • The bosses: the boss of every stage and the four of stage 4.
  • The sequences: the opening, the story, the on-board computer and the ending, animated.
  • The music: the 26 tunes, from its own sound engine.
  • The texts: every text of the cartridge, the story and the ending.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: the pause passwords, the missile of ship 3, FIGTHER, graphics nobody loads.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding the ROM, the mapper, the states, the RAM and the VRAM.

This is documentation and preservation work: the code and artwork still belong to their authors and to Konami, and the cartridge image is not distributed.
